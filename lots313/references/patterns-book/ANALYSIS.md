### Key Insights for Blocks 313

**Lot Size**
- Designed for 50ft × 100ft lots — standard Detroit lot size
- Building coverage: 22% on 50x100, 27% on 40x100
- The Linden (duplex) = 1,998 sq ft total, 1,064 sq ft footprint
- The Grove (fourplex) = 3,660 sq ft total

**Missing Middle Housing**
- Pattern book is explicitly about "missing middle" housing (duplexes, fourplexes)
- 70% of Michigan housing is single-family; household size shrank from 4.5 to 2.5
- Prior to Euclidean zoning, duplexes/fourplexes were normal — "hidden in plain sight"
- 47% of all housing units built before 1970

**Zoning Recommendations**
- Remove minimum lot width/area constraints
- Allow by-right duplex/fourplex on corner lots or throughout neighborhoods
- Eliminate parking minimums (plan shows 1 off-street space per unit max)
- Pre-approved building plans to cut permit time
- Density FAR standards should be removed

**Cost Containment**
- No basement (raised concrete block foundation instead)
- No garages
- No fireplaces or built-ins
- Must pair with landbank/low-cost land acquisition
- Land acquisition via blight removal, CDBG, brownfield, Neighborhood Enterprise Zones
- Estimate: $500K duplex / $915K fourplex (2022)

**Design Elements**
- Separate entries for each unit (dignity element)
- First-floor units fully accessible (ADA-ready)
- Sound baffling via stacked service areas
- Separate HVAC per unit
- In-unit washer/dryer
- HardiPlank/stucco/masonry — no vinyl
- Optional skins: Craftsman, Prairie, Colonial Revival

### Relevance to Blocks 313
- Detroit lot sizes match the 50x100 design assumption
- Landbank acquisition is the exact cost-containment model
- Pattern book gives us pre-approved plan sets to install in Blocks 313
- The zoning reform playbook maps directly to City of Detroit code changes
- "Critical regionalism" — the design philosophy matches our contextual approach
