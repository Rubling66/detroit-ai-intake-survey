Meeting Close & Decision Card Facilitator Guide

ISUPK 501(c)(3) Non-Profit • Mid-Michigan Outreach Operational Standard

1. Operational Purpose

The Physical Meeting Close Card is the primary conversion tool used at the conclusion of all public seminars, pop-up table presentations, and community meetings across Flint and Mid-Michigan. Its goal is to capture participant contact details and study kit requests to reach the Phase 1 target of 1,000 community engagements.

2. The 60-Second Verbal Close Script (Speaker Protocol)

Instructors and team leads should pause 10 minutes before concluding a presentation, ensure Close Cards and pens are handed to every attendee, and deliver this verbal close:

"Family, thank you for joining us today. Before we wrap up, our team is passing out the ISUPK Decision & Connection Card.

Take a moment right now to check off the boxes that apply to you. If you want us to mail you our complete printed 12-Tribes Scriptural Poster, check Box 1. If you want to receive the Zoom link for our Tuesday & Thursday live Bible classes, check Box 2. If you or your family need food or winter clothing support, check Box 3.

Fill out your name and contact info at the bottom, tear off the decision stub, and hand it to our team lead before you leave. Keep the back portion with our meeting schedule for your records!"

3. Post-Meeting Card Processing Workflow

    Step 1 — Collect Stubs: Gather all completed decision stubs at the door or table. Step 2 — Database Entry: Log the attendee details directly into the web database via http://localhost:8080/contact.html or the REST API. Step 3 — Kit Fulfillment: Mail requested study kits within 48 hours from the Detroit regional hub (16134 Moross Rd). Step 4 — Follow-Up Call/SMS: Send SMS confirmation with Zoom link prior to Tuesday/Thursday classes.
