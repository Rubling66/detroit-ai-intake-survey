About Our Educational Initiative

Providing scripture-based education, moral mentorship, and community support across Mid-Michigan under the ISUPK 501(c)(3) non-profit charter.

Our Core Mission

The Israelite School of Universal Practical Knowledge (ISUPK) operates as a faith-based 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to restoring historical identity, biblical literacy, and community strength to African American, Hispanic/Latino, and Native American populations.

Our Mid-Michigan outreach initiative focuses on Flint, Saginaw, and Genesee County, combining spiritual education with direct community support, food and clothing distribution, youth mentorship, and prison ministry.

  • ✓ Scripture-Based Education: Free Bible study classes and lineage research.
  • ✓ Community Aid: Food boxes, winter coat drives, and family assistance.
  • ✓ Rehabilitation Support: Prison ministry and mentorship for at-risk youth.

Phase 1 Regional Goal

Targeting 1,000 direct community engagements across Flint and Mid-Michigan with an initial $1,000 pilot outreach budget.
