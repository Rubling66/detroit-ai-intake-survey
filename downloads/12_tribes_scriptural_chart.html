The 12 Tribes of Israel — Scriptural & Historical Guide

ISUPK 501(c)(3) Educational Publication Series • Mid-Michigan Outreach

This reference chart details the biblical lineages of the 12 Tribes according to Genesis 49, Deuteronomy 33, and Revelation 7, connecting historical dispersons with biblical prophecy.

Biblical Tribe Scriptural Blessing / Prophecy Key Reference Passages Historical Dispersion & Lineage Notes
Judah (Yawawdah) The scepter & ruler's staff; lion of the tribe. Genesis 49:8-10, Deuteronomy 33:7 African American diaspora (Transatlantic captivity)
Benjamin (Banyaman) Ravenous wolf; beloved of the LORD dwelling in safety. Genesis 49:27, Deuteronomy 33:12 West Indian / Caribbean lineage diaspora
Levi (Lawaya) Teachers of the law & covenant; priesthood lineage. Deuteronomy 33:8-11, Malachi 2:4-7 Haitian diaspora & priestly heritage
Ephraim (Ahparayam) Fruitful bough; leader of the Northern Kingdom. Genesis 49:22-26, Hosea 7:8 Puerto Rican heritage lineage
Manasseh (Manashah) Ten thousands of Ephraim & thousands of Manasseh. Deuteronomy 33:17, Genesis 48:19 Cuban heritage lineage
Simeon (Shamawan) Brethren in instruments of cruelty; scattered in Israel. Genesis 49:5-7 Dominican Republic heritage lineage
Zebulun (Zabawlawan) Dwelling at the haven of the sea; shipping havens. Genesis 49:13, Deuteronomy 33:18 Guatemalan & Panamanian lineages
Gad (Gad) A troop shall overcome him, but he overcomes at last. Genesis 49:19, Deuteronomy 33:20 North American Native Tribal Nations
Reuben (Rawaban) Unstable as water; dignity & power. Genesis 49:3-4, Deuteronomy 33:6 Seminole Indian lineage
Asher (Ashar) Bread shall be fat; royal dainties & rich lands. Genesis 49:20, Deuteronomy 33:24 Colombian, Venezuelan & Incan lineages
Naphtali (Naphatalay) Hind let loose; giveth goodly words. Genesis 49:21, Deuteronomy 33:23 Hawaiian & Polynesian lineages
Issachar (Yashashakar) Strong ass couching down between two burdens. Genesis 49:14-15, Deuteronomy 33:18 Mexican & Aztec heritage lineage
Key Scriptural Text: "Hear, LORD, the voice of Judah, and bring him unto his people: let his hands be sufficient for him; and be thou an help to him from his enemies." — Deuteronomy 33:7
