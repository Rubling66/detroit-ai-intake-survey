ISUPK Prison Ministry & Re-Entry Initiative • 501(c)(3) Non-Profit

1. Message of Transformation & Hope

Our prison ministry and re-entry outreach programs provide scriptural guidance, moral discipline, and brotherhood support for individuals inside correctional facilities and those transitioning back into community life in Mid-Michigan.

"To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death..." — Psalm 102:20

2. Key Principles for Spiritual & Social Rehabilitation

  • Break Free from Recidivism: Developing personal responsibility, moral discipline, and work ethic.
  • Scripture as Mental Fortification: Replacing destructive street mindsets with biblical principles of manhood and family leadership.
  • Substance & Addiction Recovery: Cleansing the body and mind according to scriptural health laws (1 Corinthians 3:16-17).
  • Re-entry Support Network: Connecting formerly incarcerated individuals with housing advice, employment assistance, and brotherhood mentorship upon release in Flint and Detroit.
