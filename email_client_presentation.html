501(c)(3) Non-Profit Outreach Strategy

Social Media Traction & Growth Proposal

Mid-Michigan Educational Outreach Initiative (Flint / Saginaw / Genesee County)

Executive Briefing & Core Objective

Dear Director & Project Client,

This executive proposal outlines our complete campaign framework engineered to accomplish your primary goal: driving high-volume traction, active followers, and live engagement directly to your existing social media channels (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram) while engaging 1,000 local community members in Mid-Michigan.

📞 60-Second Verbal Script (Client Phone Explanation)

"Our entire system is built as a social media traction engine. We aren't replacing your existing pages—we are supercharging them. We use short-form video snippets and geotargeted local ads that push traffic directly to your Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram accounts. Every physical flyer, pop-up table banner, and meeting close card features a prominent QR code taking people directly to your YouTube Live stream or Facebook page. When people request our free 12-Tribes study guides, they are prompted to subscribe for live Tuesday & Thursday night classes, building a steady pipeline of 1,000 engaged local followers."

The 3-Stage Social Media Growth Funnel

STAGE 1: Attract & Broadcast (Top of Funnel)
• Geotargeted social ads ($300 allocation) targeting Flint, Saginaw & Genesee County.
• Short 60-second video clips (Reels, Shorts) featuring Bible study teasers.
• In-person pop-up info tables on S. Saginaw St capturing local foot traffic.
STAGE 2: Convert to Social Followers (Middle of Funnel)
• QR Code Bridge: Scan QR codes on physical flyers & meeting close cards to land directly on YouTube/Facebook.
• Download Incentive: Free 12-Tribes PDF downloads require social channel subscriptions.
• Automated form confirmation emails sending direct Zoom & stream links.
STAGE 3: Retention & Live Engagement (Bottom of Funnel)
• Weekly Tuesday & Thursday 7:00 PM EST live Zoom & YouTube streams.
• Interactive live Q&A chat building repeat audience watch time.
• Monthly community food & clothing drives generating user content.
